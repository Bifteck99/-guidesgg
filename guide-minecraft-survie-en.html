// Minecraft Guide — Survival

Progression Day 1 → Day 30

From spawn to late game: every step in order, with the priorities that actually matter.

Difficulty Normal / Hard
Version 1.21+
Read time ~15 min
Early D1–D10 Mid D11–D21 Late D22–D27 Endgame D28–D30
Early Game
Day 1Spawn & Surviving Night 1Goal: survive the first night▼

Actions

  • Chop wood right away — 32 to 64 blocks is enough to get started
  • Craft a workbench, wooden tools, then stone tools as fast as possible
  • Kill sheep for a bed — absolute priority to skip the night
  • Mine coal for torches + furnace
  • Build a temporary shelter before nightfall (even a 5×5 box)
  • Secure food: pigs, cows, chickens near spawn

End of day checklist

Stone tools crafted
Bed placed inside shelter
Stable food supply (≥10 items)
Shelter closed with a door
The bed is the most important item on Day 1. No bed = you endure the night. With a bed = you skip it and keep your momentum.
D2 – D3Surface Mining & IronGoal: full iron armor + shield▼
  • Explore surface caves for iron — faster than strip mining
  • Craft the shield first as soon as you have iron: the most cost-effective early game item
  • Aim for full iron armor before going deeper
  • Water buckets: essential for falls and lava

Iron craft priority

  • 1. Shield → 2. Pickaxe → 3. Sword → 4. Armor
The shield blocks 100% of projectile damage. It's the best early game defense, far better than a full armor set.
D4 – D5Base & Stable ResourcesGoal: food autonomy + exploration▼
  • Set up a wheat, carrot, or potato farm near your base
  • Breed cows/sheep for leather and wool
  • Explore within a 500-block radius: villages, caves, useful biomes
  • Note important coordinates
A nearby village is a massive accelerator. Librarian villagers can give you Mending and Fortune long before you'd find them naturally.
D6 – D7Diamond MiningGoal: diamond pickaxe + enchanting table▼
  • Dig down to Y = -58: optimal layer since 1.18
  • Strip mine: tunnels spaced 2 blocks apart
  • Minimum goal: 5 diamonds (pickaxe + enchanting table)

Diamond usage priority

  • 1. Diamond pickaxe (3) → 2. Enchanting table (2 + obsidian) → 3. Sword → 4. Full armor
Y=-58 is in the deepslate layers where diamonds are 2× more frequent than the old Y=11 meta.
D8 – D10Nether PreparationGoal: portal open + first enchant▼
  • Craft obsidian (10 blocks) and open the portal
  • Get first enchantments on your sword or pickaxe
  • Farm some basic XP

Checklist before entering the Nether

Full iron armor minimum
Shield in inventory
Bow + 64 arrows
Food × 2 stacks
Mid Game
D11 – D14Nether ExplorationGoal: Blaze rods + Ender Pearls▼
  • Find a Nether Fortress by following X coordinates
  • Farm Blazes for Blaze Rods
  • Get Ender Pearls via Endermen or Piglins
  • Bring back Nether Wart for potions
The Nether Fortress is the key mid-game milestone. Without Blaze Powder, you can't craft Eyes of Ender to find the Stronghold.
D15 – D18Villager TradingGoal: Mending + Fortune + Protection IV▼
  • Reset Librarian villagers until you get the enchants you want
  • Priority: Mending, Fortune III, Protection IV
  • Build a basic XP farm
With Mending, your tools never break again. With Fortune III, each diamond vein gives 3× more drops.
D19 – D21Full Diamond + TriangulationGoal: find the Stronghold▼
  • Complete diamond armor with serious enchants
  • Brew first potions
  • Craft Eyes of Ender and triangulate the Stronghold

Triangulation method

  • Throw an Eye → note the direction → move 500 blocks → throw a second Eye → dig down at the intersection
Late Game
D22 – D24End PreparationGoal: activate the End portal▼

Pre-End checklist

Enchanted diamond armor (Protection IV min)
Diamond sword (Sharpness IV+)
Bow + 128+ arrows
Water bucket + food × 3 stacks
64 blocks to climb pillars
Slow Falling potion (recommended)
Day 25Ender DragonGoal: kill the Dragon▼
  • Destroy End Crystals on pillars first — they regenerate the Dragon
  • Attack the Dragon during its transition phases above the portal
  • Avoid the purple breath (End Acid) on the ground
This is technically the end of the "official" game progression. But for most players, this is where it really begins.
D26 – D27End Cities & ElytraGoal: get the Elytra▼
  • Use the End gateway to teleport to the outer End Islands
  • Find an End Ship — the Elytra is inside
  • Collect Shulker Boxes: portable storage
Elytra + firework rockets = infinite movement. It's the single item that changes gameplay the most. Absolute priority.
Endgame
D28 – D30Netherite & Automatic FarmsGoal: max gear + automation▼
  • Mine in the Nether at Y = 14 for Ancient Debris
  • Upgrade armor + tools to Netherite
  • Prepare the Beacon (kill the Wither)

Farms to build first

  • Iron Farm — unlimited iron, the foundation of everything
  • Gold Farm (Nether) — passive gold + XP
  • Gunpowder Farm — for Elytra rockets
  • Sugar Cane Farm — for enchantment books
From Day 30 onward, the direction is entirely up to you: redstone, mega-builds, PvP... Farms are the foundation of all of it.
