Trophy pushing is the main progression system in Brawl Stars. Each brawler has its own counter, and the goal is to accumulate trophies to unlock rewards, set personal records, or climb the global leaderboards. Since the 2024-2025 updates, the system is far more strategic than it looks.

01

// The basics

The Trophy System

Each brawler has its own independent trophy counter. Win = gain trophies, lose = lose trophies. But the gain/loss ratio shifts significantly at each threshold.

Official thresholds

0 → 250 trophies
Very fast progression ✓
250 → 500
Easy, good gain/loss ratio ✓
500 → 750
Still gaining more than losing ✓
750 → 1,000
Balanced — stronger opponents
1,000 (Max Tier)
Main milestone — Season Trophies unlocked
1,000 → 2,000
Losing more than gaining ✗
2,000 (Prestige 2)
Bot matches off · Win Streak off
3,000+
World elite — very hard
Every 1,000 after
New Prestige tier every thousand
Max Tier at 1,000: the main goal for most players. Beyond that, trophies become "Season Trophies" filling the Trophy Box — a cosmetic reward box (skins, gems) that resets each season.

The Prestige System

Since the 2025 update, each player has a Prestige level visible as a badge on their profile. A new Prestige tier unlocks every 1,000 trophies beyond Max Tier. Unlike trophies that reset each season, Prestige is permanent — it's the new status symbol in Brawl Stars.

The new MMR matchmaking

Since early 2025, matchmaking no longer relies solely on brawler trophies. A hidden MMR takes your overall skill level into account to balance matches. Two players with the same trophy count won't necessarily face the same opponents.

What this means: you can have 800 trophies on a brawler but still face much stronger players. Your MMR remembers your level even when you switch brawlers.
02

// Progressing efficiently

Best Methods

🎮

1. Pick the right mode for your brawler

Solo Showdown: very effective for solo players — fast matches, no teammate dependency.
Brawl Ball: excellent with an organized team, short decisive games.
Gem Grab: more stable for experienced players wanting consistent gains.
The choice depends on your brawler and the current map rotation.

🗺️

2. Adapt your brawler to the map

Fast-climbing players check the map rotation before launching:
— Snipers (Piper, Belle) on open maps
— Tanks (Bull, Frank) on closed/corridor maps
— Controllers (Sprout, Gene) on defensive objective modes

👥

3. Play as a team whenever possible

Solo pushing is tough at high level due to MMR. Most competitive players prefer pushing in a trio to reduce randomness.

🎯

4. Master a few brawlers rather than all

Constantly switching is the most common mistake. An expert on 3 brawlers will always climb faster than an average player on the full roster.

⚡

5. Take advantage of Win Streak

Chaining consecutive wins gives bonus trophies. Note: the Win Streak bonus is disabled beyond 2,000 trophies (Prestige 2).

The secret of the best pushers: they analyze the map rotation at the start of each session and choose their brawler accordingly. The map dictates the brawler, not personal preference.
03

// The invisible part

Mental Game

Trophy pushing is as much about mental strength as skill. Long losing streaks — "tilt" — can erase hours of progress in just a few games.

🛑

Stop after 2-3 losses

The most important rule. Playing on tilt = losing much faster than you gain. Put the game down, come back later.

🔄

Switch mode or brawler

If a mode isn't working tonight, switch. If a brawler keeps losing, try another. Flexibility protects your progress.

📊

Set a session goal

"+50 trophies tonight" beats "I'll play until I win." A clear goal stops you from over-playing.

🧘

The season reset is your friend

Brawlers above 1,000 trophies reset to 1,000 each season. Past mistakes don't weigh on you indefinitely.

Watch out for MMR after a bad streak: even if you quit and come back, your MMR remembers recent performances. Better to end a session on a win.
04

// Two different systems

Trophies vs Ranked

Many players confuse trophy pushing with Ranked mode. They are two completely separate systems.

🏆 Trophy Pushing

  • No draft, no ban/pick
  • No imposed team composition
  • Progress brawler by brawler
  • Monthly reset above 1,000
  • Thresholds every 250 up to 1,000
  • Prestige every 1,000 after that
  • Goal: records, leaderboards
  • Win Streak bonus active (under 2,000)

⚔️ Ranked Mode

  • Ban phase + pick phase (draft)
  • Strategic team composition
  • Progress through ranks (Bronze → Legendary)
  • Seasons with exclusive rewards
  • Goal: highest rank possible
  • More strategic and demanding
  • No Win Streak bonus
Which one to choose? Team skill and draft strategy → Ranked. Solo progression, personal records or leaderboards → Trophy pushing.

In Summary

Trophy pushing in 2025 is far more strategic than before. MMR matchmaking, thresholds at 250 / 500 / 750 / 1,000 then every thousand, and map rotation all change the game. The players who progress fastest choose their modes smartly, master a few brawlers, play as a team, and know when to stop.
