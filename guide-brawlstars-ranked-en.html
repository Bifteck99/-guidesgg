Mythic is the rank where most players get stuck. Not because they play badly — but because ranked at this level requires real thinking before the game even starts. The draft phase (ban + pick) can decide 40% of the outcome.

01

// First step

Ban Phase

Who to ban first

The ban serves two purposes: eliminate a brawler that's unplayable on the current map, or eliminate a brawler so strong in the meta that the game becomes unbalanced.

🗺️

Brawlers unplayable on certain maps

A melee brawler like Edgar is useless on an open map — but dangerous on a closed map with corridors. Ban what's strong on this specific map, not in general.

📊

Dominant meta brawlers

Some brawlers dominate the meta regardless of the map. In 2025, Draco is the prime example: mobility + damage + sustain. Win rate > 55% and ban rate > 30% = auto-ban.

⚠️

Exception: you want to play that brawler

If your main is on the priority ban list — don't ban them. Let the opponent do it. Focus your ban on the second most dangerous brawler.

// Second step

Pick Phase

The 3 criteria in order

🗺️

1. Adapt your brawler to the map

This is criterion #1. Edgar on an open map can never close the gap. Piper in a closed corridor can never aim. Check the map before everything else.

⚔️

2. Counter the enemy composition

If they have 2 tanks, pick a brawler with piercing damage. If they have 2 snipers, pick a mobile brawler. Counter-compose when you can.

🎮

3. Play a brawler you master

The best brawler in the game played without experience is worth less than a B-tier brawler you know by heart.

// In-game

Strategies by Mode

Mechanics change by mode. Understanding the real objective of each mode — not just "get kills" — is what separates Mythic from Legendary.

Brawl BallFootball / Hockey Brawl▼

Brawl Ball is a position war. The goal is to control zones to advance toward the enemy goal.

📏

Respect your lane

Each player has a responsibility zone. Grouping up = leaving a flank open = conceding a goal.

🧱

Position before engaging

Don't rush the ball carrier without a favorable angle. A bad engagement = 3 seconds of respawn = goal conceded.

⚡

Super = instant opening

Save your super to create a goal opening or defend a critical situation.

HeistSafe Attack & Defense▼

The goal is to damage the enemy safe. Approach using walls as cover — don't expose yourself unnecessarily.

⚡ The Hypercharge

Hypercharge lasts ~5 seconds and massively boosts damage, speed and abilities. In Heist, a well-timed Hypercharge can destroy 30% of the enemy safe. Don't waste it on pointless kills.

Gem Grab / Hot ZoneZone Control▼

These modes share the same core: control the center. It's a war of attrition.

Assassin comp: charge supers on the edges, dive together to eliminate the enemy team and take the center.
Control comp: block the center with zone damage, force the enemy into the poison late game.
04

// To wrap up

Final Tips

👥

Play in duo or trio

Solo ranked depends too much on random teammates. A friend to communicate with doubles your win rate. Use "Quick Join" or find teammates on Brawl Stars Discord servers.

🎯

Specialize in 3 brawlers max

Trying to play every brawler = mastering none. Pick 2-3 versatile brawlers and build your draft around them.

📈

Analyze your losses

After each loss, ask one question: was it the draft, positioning, or super management? Identify ONE problem per session.

🧠

Don't play on tilt

After 2 consecutive losses, stop. Tilt makes you lose ranks much faster than skill makes you gain them.

📱

Use Brawlify

brawlify.com gives real-time win rates and ban rates by brawler and map. Check before every ranked session.

🏆

Consistency above all

1 hour of focused ranked beats 4 hours of casual play. Set a session goal and stop when you reach it or after 2 losses.

The real secret: the difference between Mythic and Legendary isn't mechanical skill — it's consistent decision-making. Coherent bans, adapted picks, respecting the composition. Apply these principles every single game.
